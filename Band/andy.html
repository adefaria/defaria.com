Equipment & Stage Rig

People ask me what I think about when I'm playing. Well, I think about what I'm playing, of course. Then I think about how much I'm getting paid. Then with some quick arithmetic I determine how much per note I'm getting paid and adjust my playing accordingly!

I play a Steinberger guitar mainly and an Ovation acoustic. I use a Korg A3 effects processor that our Atari STacy MIDI sequencer switches automatically at the right time, paired with ADA speakers and amps. I really like the Steinberger, though it must be an irregular because I haven't been able to find the headpiece since I bought it! Luckily it doesn't go out of tune often.
