Mike Fraser

← Back to Cast of Shadows

Mike Fraser played primarily bass in the band (occasionally acoustic guitar) and was our lead vocalist as well as keyboardist. This was made possible by the fact that the band ran a MIDI sequencer to play all the keyboard parts as well as a sampler—and even ran the light show on occasion!

Equipment & Stage Rig

Well let's see... my finger strikes the string and the magnetic coil picks up the vibes—it goes through the wire and into the box... and then, a low rumble comes out of a cardboard speaker. Ha! Moosic! Actually, if you want to be more technical, I use Fender bass guitars, a Peavey amp with Acoustic speakers, and Kawai & Korg keyboards (that I hardly ever play because the computer does that!). Too bad we couldn't roll a Baby Grand on stage every night! (I probably wouldn't play it either). When do I get paid!?!?
