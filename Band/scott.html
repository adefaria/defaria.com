Scott Dinn

← Back to Cast of Shadows

Scott Dinn was the drummer, and he had his hands full. Scott was responsible for running the sequencer and staying locked in time with that ever-present click track in his monitor. Scott also had a bag of tricks in a rack including a sampler and a DrumKAT which he used to trigger sounds—both musical and some not-so-musical ones like samples of Beavis and Butt-Head!

Equipment & Stage Rig

Hmmmm, where do I start?? Do I tell you about my 9-piece Tama Granstar Custom drums in a Cherry Wine finish with lots of Zildjian cymbals, or do I first tell you about my electronic gear like my Atari STacy laptop computer used to sequence all the keyboards, lights, and signal processors, and my drumKAT triggering an Alesis D4 drum module and a Peavey SP sampler to get all those crazy sounds... I just don't know where to begin! Maybe I should tell you about the ProMark 747 rock nylon-tip oak sticks I use? What about all the gates, compressors, microphones, mixer, and miles of MIDI cables that I use to keep track of all my drums and sound modules? Let's just say I have a lot of stuff! Anyone wanna help me tear down tonight!?!?
