Speak Lite Privacy Policy

100% On-Device Speech Synthesis • Zero Analytics • No Ads

Effective Date: October 17, 2026
Developer: Andrew DeFaria (Andrew@DeFaria.com)

1. Overview

Speak Lite (com.defaria.speak.lite) is a hands-free notification and clipboard text-to-speech utility for Android. Speak Lite performs all notification processing and speech synthesis locally on your device using the Android system Text-to-Speech engine. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data with third parties.

2. Permissions & Local Processing

  • Notification Listener Service (android.service.notification.NotificationListenerService): Used only when explicitly enabled by the user to read aloud incoming notifications from user-selected applications. Notification text is processed locally on-device and stored only in your local on-device speech history log.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Your speech history log and app preferences are stored exclusively on your Android device. You may clear your speech history log at any time inside the app or by uninstalling the application.
