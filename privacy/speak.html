Speak Pro & Speak Lite Privacy Policy

Zero Third-Party Analytics • No Advertising • User-Controlled Speech Synthesis

Effective Date: October 17, 2026
Developer: Andrew DeFaria (Andrew@DeFaria.com)

1. Overview

Speak Pro (com.defaria.speakpro) and Speak Lite (com.defaria.speaklite) are hands-free notification and text-to-speech utility applications for Android. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties, advertisers, or analytics networks.

2. Permissions & Data Processing

  • Notification Listener Service (android.service.notification.NotificationListenerService): Used only when explicitly enabled by the user to read aloud incoming notifications from user-selected apps (such as Messages, Email, or Tasks). Notification text is processed transiently for speech synthesis and stored only in the local on-device speech history log.
  • Network Access (INTERNET): In Speak Pro, when the user configures a personal TTS synthesis server endpoint, notification text is sent over HTTPS exclusively to the user's configured synthesis server to generate speech audio. Speak Lite performs speech synthesis locally on-device using the Android system TextToSpeech engine.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Your speech history log and app preferences are stored locally on your Android device. You can clear the speech log at any time using the "Clear Log" button in the app or by uninstalling the application.
